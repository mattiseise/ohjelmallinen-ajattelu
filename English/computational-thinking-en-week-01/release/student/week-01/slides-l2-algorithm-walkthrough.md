# Worked example — instant noodles

We will walk one familiar task through the four parts of an algorithm.

The same template works for a recipe, a route, a help-desk script.

---

## Goal

A bowl of hot noodles, ready to eat in five minutes.

What you want at the end.

---

## Input

- A kettle
- Water
- One packet of instant noodles
- A bowl
- A fork

What you must have before you start.

---

## Steps

1. Fill the kettle with water to the line on the side.
2. Switch the kettle on and wait until it clicks off.
3. Open the noodle packet and put the noodles in the bowl.
4. Pour the boiled water over the noodles until the bowl is two-thirds full.
5. Cover the bowl with a plate and wait four minutes.
6. Stir with the fork.

The actions, in order.

---

## Output

A bowl of hot noodles, ready to eat.

What you have at the end.

---

## The template scales

**Goal · input · steps · output** works for a recipe, a bus route, a help-desk reply or a morning routine.

Naming the parts makes the algorithm easier to test, easier to share, and easier for someone else to follow.
