# 2-minute presentation rubric

Lesson 24 — AT3 oral component

A short reminder deck the teacher can project between presentations.

---

## What to say (4 beats)

1. **What** the program does — one or two sentences in plain English.
2. **How** it does it — variables, `if/else`, loop (name the four parts if there is one).
3. **What went wrong** — the one bug, by name (e.g. *"I had a `scre` instead of `score`"*).
4. **How you fixed it** — the one line you changed.

---

## Timing — 2 minutes

Roughly **30 seconds per beat.**

- Use a phone timer visible to the room.
- Under-time is fine; over-time is not.
- About 200 spoken words total.

---

## Pass criteria

The teacher checks:

- The program **runs** (or the failed attempt is documented with the error message).
- The student can **name** in plain English what the program does.
- One **bug-and-fix pair** is described.
- The **2-minute** presentation was delivered.

Four checks. Any one missing → not-yet-pass; one more thing to fix.

---

## What is *not* required

- **Perfect code.** Working is enough.
- **Fancy slides.** No slides at all is fine.
- **A memorised script.** Talk in your own words.
- **An elegant variable name.** Clear is enough.

---

## After each presentation

One peer feedback question, asked of the room:

> **"Name one thing you noticed."**

Short, specific, kind. One sentence is plenty.

Then on to the next presenter.
